📊 Customer Performance Dashboard & Behavioral Analytics

📌 Project Overview
The Customers Performance Dashboard is an end-to-end analytics solution designed to analyze customer demographics, spending behaviors, regional performance, and profiling segmentation. This dashboard provides strategic insights into age-based revenue streams, family demographics, top-value buyers, and gender parity to support targeted marketing and sales strategies.

📊 Key Dashboard Components & Features
1. High-Level Core KPIs:
Average Customer Age: Establishes the baseline age distribution of the active customer base.
Total Customer Count: Tracks total customer volume across all operational regions.

2. Demographic & Family Structure Analysis:
Family Status Breakdown: Compares spending patterns and total revenue generated by customers with children versus those without.
Geographical Insights: Highlights top-performing countries for each family demographic segment.

3. Revenue Segmentation by Age Group:
Age Bracket Evaluation: Analyzes revenue generation across multi-generational age groups against an average performance benchmark line.
Target Demographic Identification: Pinpoints the primary age groups driving the majority of overall revenue.

4. Customer Profiling & Lifecycle Segmentation:
Tiered Segmentation: Categorizes the customer base into distinct lifecycle tiers—Loyal Customers, Periodic Buyers, and VIP Customers.
Value Distribution: Evaluates capital contribution from each customer group to prioritize loyalty initiatives.

5. High-Value Buyer Leaderboard:
Top Customer Ranking: Features a visual leaderboard showcasing top-ranked individual buyers sorted by overall spending.

7. Gender Earnings Distribution:
Parity Analysis: Measures revenue share and spending balance between male and female customer segments.
