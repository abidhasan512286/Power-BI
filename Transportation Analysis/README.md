🚍 Voyage Line City Transit Analytics Dashboard

📌 Project Overview
The Voyage Line City Transit Analytics Dashboard is an end-to-end business intelligence and urban mobility solution designed to evaluate transit ridership, fleet operational efficiency, route profitability, and passenger demographics.
This multi-report dashboard equips transit planners and city officials with actionable insights to optimize bus schedules, balance fleet utilization, improve revenue generation, 
and understand commuting patterns across different rider profiles.

📊 Key Dashboard Pages & Features

📄 Report 01: Executive Overview & Rider Demographics
    High-Level Transit KPIs: Tracks core performance metrics including Total Ridership, Average Riders Per Trip, Total Revenue, and Total Trips.
    Route Distribution: Analyzes passenger shares across major routes (e.g., East-West Express, Central Line, Airport Express, University Line, South Line).
    Fleet Benchmarking (TopN / BottomN Analysis): Features dynamic parametric filtering to identify top-performing and under-performing bus units for targeted maintenance and route re-allocation.

  Demographic Profiling:
    Age & Gender Breakdown: Evaluates average rider age and spending distribution across gender categories.
    Occupational Commute Analysis: Categorizes riders by occupation (Professional, Self-Employed, Unemployed, Retired, Student) and maps occupation-specific peak travel hours.

📄 Report 02: Operations & Temporal Dynamics
    Intraday Ridership Timeline: Tracks real-time passenger trends throughout operating hours to highlight exact Peak and Down/Off-Peak operational hours.
    Time-Range Distribution: Segments ridership density into standard 3-hour operational blocks (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Late Night).
    Weekly & Monthly Trajectory: Visualizes passenger volume trends across weekdays (Sunday to Saturday) and monitors Year-over-Year (YoY) performance change.
    Fleet Utilization Matrix: Classifies transit buses into three operational health categories—Properly Utilized, Overutilized, and Underutilized—to assist in load-balancing.
    Route Highlights: Instant callouts for the Busiest Route vs. Least Busy Route.

🛠️ Interactive Features & Navigation
    Multi-Page Navigation: Quick toggles between Report 01 (Demographics & Financials) and Report 02 (Operations & Timings).
    Dynamic Slicers: Global filters for Gender and Route, plus dynamic TopN/BottomN rank parameters.
    Theme Support: Clean Dark Theme architecture with built-in Light/Dark theme toggle support.

🛠️ Tech Stack & Tools
    BI & Visualization: Power BI
    Data Processing & Analytics: DAX and Power Query
    Data Source: CSV
    Data Domain: Public Transportation & Urban Mobility Operations
    Bancground & UI design: Microsoft PowerPoint
