🛠️ Detailed Project Breakdown
1. Voyage Line City Transit Analytics Dashboard
Domain: Public Transportation & Transit Operations

Key Features:
Intraday & Operational Timelines: Analyzes ridership trajectory across operating hours to identify peak and off-peak travel times.
Fleet Health & Utilization: Categorizes transit vehicles into Properly Utilized, Overutilized, and Underutilized states for load balancing.
Demographics & Commute Mapping: Maps ridership by occupation profiles and evaluates age/gender distribution across busiest routes.

2. Patients Emergency Room Visit Report
Domain: Healthcare Management & Clinical Operations

Key Features:
Operational Metrics: Tracks total patient visits, appointment types (Administrative vs. Non-Administrative), and admission channels (Walk-ins vs. Referrals).
Patient Experience & Bottlenecks: Evaluates average wait times and unrated service ratios to streamline emergency workflow.
Demographic Satisfaction Matrix: Interactive heatmap cross-analyzing patient race and age brackets to measure satisfaction parity.

3. Customers Performance Dashboard
Domain: Customer Analytics & Retail Intelligence
Key Features:

Revenue Segmentation: Evaluates age-group revenue benchmarks and highlights high-performing customer segments.
Demographics & Family Impact: Compares spending patterns between customers with vs. without children across operational regions.
Lifecycle Profiling: Classifies buyers into Loyal, Periodic, and VIP categories alongside an Esteemed Buyers leaderboard.

4. Bank Loan Analysis & Risk Dashboard
Domain: Financial Services & Credit Risk

Key Features:
Financial KPIs: Monitors total applications, funded amounts, cash recovery, average interest rates, and borrower DTI ratios.
Portfolio Health: Segmented risk analysis categorizing loans into Good Loans (Fully Paid/Current) vs. Bad Loans (Charged Off).
Geographical & Category Insights: Features dynamic US regional mapping, loan term breakdowns, and purpose distribution treemaps.
